# Pokémon Chuyển Sinh v1.5.33 — Tranh Trứng: sealed eggs and end-game hatch reveal

Working branch: `feature/lan-local-beta`, 2026-10-28; `main` untouched.

## Player-approved mechanic
- Tranh Trứng (4 trainers including AI): a collected egg stays a **closed egg**, sliding **close to the owner** like the older Egg Rush. NO 45-second incubation, Pokémon support AI, combat skills, HP bar or Walk animation for these eggs while playing. That reduces the number of active companion Animators in this mode.
- Co-op / Sống Sót and Sống Còn: keep the **real directional Walk/Idle support Pokémon**, 60% visual scale, 85% trainer move speed, real Host-synced HP and previous Rift fix. Their normal eggs still incubate and hatch as supports. Do not mix these mechanics.
- Egg Rush finishes once when **10 eggs are successfully collected across all four trainers** or **12-minute** deadline occurs. A trainer owning 6 eggs does **NOT** win early; they still might lose to another trainer with fewer but higher-value eggs. Respawn remains 8 seconds.
- Only at end, all collected eggs hatch visually in the result panel. A Pokémon scores **Mew #151 = 20**, **Ditto #132 = 10**, **all other Pokémon = 5**. Pre-existing rarity choices retained as **4% Mew, 8% Ditto, 88% ordinary** (Host commits deterministic egg identity at pickup, not after result).
- The **Host alone** computes totals from identities sealed at pickup. During play `er:take` packets carry Pokémon dex **0**; no client learns the Pokémon or point value until receiving the final result. Each final standing has trainer name, trainer Pokémon sprite, number of eggs, `hatched: [{id,dex,points}]`, and sum `points`. Sort by **points DESC**, then eggs DESC, then pickup timestamp ASC/name/UID for deterministic ties. Never use eggs count as the primary winner metric.
- End results initially display trainers sorted by **provisional egg count** with hidden rank badges and zero points. Eggs visibly hatch one by one into real Pokémon sprite canvases (no fake names), +20/+10/+5 appears and the running tally increments. After the LAST egg, reveal the Host-authoritative #1–#4 ranking and champion/title. This timing also prevents the standard game-over title from spoiling the winner prematurely.
- Existing 4-player result portrait icons, mobile scrolling, personal Shiny, saved progression, scoreboards and AI stat balance remain; other modes' results untouched.
