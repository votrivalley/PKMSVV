# 1.5.23 — Survival ranking, varied AI strength and rescue QA

Sống Còn shows own health plus a Survival ranking, without the nine other
trainers' health bars, teammate arrows or the irrelevant Egg Rush support table.
Living trainers rank above eliminated trainers; status labels are “Sống” / “Ngủm”.
The panel width follows trainer names rather than reserving an empty column.
The final point formula and winner rule are unchanged. The ranking fits
portrait and landscape screens below own health/buffs.

Sống Sót keeps teammate health bars and a compact local support count below
their actual bounds. Long names fit the name column; offscreen markers are
kept out of that sidebar. Tranh Trứng retains its own egg scoreboard.

## Why Royale bots were weak

The combat positioning condition only handled following the trainer or nearby
Co-op roaming. Royale bots had a separate roaming goal, so they never entered
this ranged/melee spacing branch. They now keep appropriate attack range and
retreat farther when under 40% HP, including away from a contested egg when hurt.

Each Royale bot independently rolls normal (25%), trained (45%) or elite (30%).
Profiles use `G.Upgrades.perks` and the same capped HP, attack, defense, cooldown,
speed and regeneration upgrades available to humans. Species stats, type rules,
legal move offers, initial run level 1 and two-support limit remain intact. No
trainer save is edited, and bots remain mortal. Co-op/Egg Rush do not gain these
Royale-only starting profiles. Difficulty is not copied from the trainer.
