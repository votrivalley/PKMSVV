# Pokémon Chuyển Sinh v1.5.35 — chapter completion purple Gacha tickets

Date: 28 October 2026. Working branch `feature/lan-local-beta`; `main` remains untouched.

## Player request and accepted implementation

- To reduce early-game frustration, award **one purple Gacha ×10 ticket per chapter**. "Purple" is the actual `ticket10` icon, stored at **`G.DB.save.tickets.t10`**, **not** the yellow single-pull ticket `tickets.t1` or coins.
- Available story: **Chapter 0, 1, 2, 3**. Up to **four purple tickets** for a new save, awarded once each; a ticket can be spent on the normal ten-pull Gacha UI.
- Chapter 0 award requires that the Chapter 0 story was completed (or validly skipped) **AND the starter was chosen**. A skipped personality quiz must still display starter choice, not award before picking a Pokémon.
- Chapter 1–3 awards require their own persistent `pkmsvvChapterNSeen` flags after first actual chapter completion. Marked "Pending" does **not** count.
- The new persistent `pkmsvvChapterPurpleRewards` map records chapter ids 0..3 already awarded; repeated cutscene/Memory replay, main-menu reentry, browser refresh and restarting the app do not award again.
- For existing saves with completed chapters from prior releases, first main-menu entry claims any eligible but unclaimed rewards **retroactively**, preserving all existing Gacha balances, other tickets, Shiny/ticket ownership, Pokémon and chapter progress.
- Reward is credited to the **local current player's save only**, then written with `G.DB.commit(true)` and the pre-existing per-device ticket ledger. Chapter rewards do not change competitive room rewards or another person's save.
- One toast notifies +1 purple Gacha ×10 or bundled +N retrospective chapter tickets. The chapter's original cutscene, starter selection, replay and chapter unlock conditions are unchanged.
- The core DB merge performs a union of chapter reward flags on save import, preserving already-claimed status. This is offline-first and does not imply cross-device cloud synchronization or prevent two disconnected saves from earning the same chapter independently before a later import. Avoid promising online account enforcement.
