## Competitive stat normalization

In **Tranh Trứng and Sống Còn only**, all trainers (real humans and AI) have the **same effective start-of-match combat-stat vector derived from the strongest actual human**. A single strongest-human snapshot is chosen at match startup from per-member bounded combat metadata (including remote Wi-Fi guests); AI cannot define the reference. Normalize **max HP, ATK, speed, cooldown, damage reduction, regeneration and both base defenses** to that vector on each run-only Player actor, on Host AND Guest after `G.Coop.begin`. No extra 2–3% AI advantage, no random Royale AI stat tiers. Keep species identity, types, skills and abilities, positional skill, and moves differing as normal gameplay mechanics; equal combat stats does not mean identical move kits. No save, permanent upgrade sheet, global DEX or account changes. Idempotent by actor, avoid repeated HP/stat scaling on reconnection. Sống Sót Co-op retains the previous challenging-AI/difficulty logic.
