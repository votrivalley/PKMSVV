# Pokémon Chuyển Sinh v1.5.32 — Rift / red circle / support HP / Orb QA

Branch: `feature/lan-local-beta`; date: 2026-10-28. Gameplay builds must be from the **same HEAD commit**, never reuse an old artifact when a run fails. `main` stays untouched.

## Requested / implemented changes

1. **Sống Còn red circle after boss Rift:** Upstream `js/systems/rift.js` returned from the boss arena without `G.World.setArena(null)`, leaving a stale non-null `G.World.arena`. The previous `G.Arena.exempt()` treated it as still in the Rift, hiding the red boundary and suspending its damage forever. Clear upstream arena on Rift exit and derive ring exemption only from live `G.Rift.inArena` / Judgment state. Multiplayer circle is hidden only inside the real Rift, resumes afterward with retained time/same authoritative center.
2. **Wider red circle at entry:** Royale radius **1900** for first 4 min, **1350** at minute 8, **900** at minute 12 and thereafter; linear transitions. Egg Rush still fixed radius **1600** and no circle damage; Co-op remains unbounded. The Royale rule remains **10-second outside grace, then 1% max HP/s**, assessed by Host. No per-player center or ungrounded camera-based ring.
3. **Support nests in Sống Còn:** Exactly **one newly offered nest each 30 seconds**, starting at second 30; up to **3 unclaimed** visible nests, each expires after 180s. No prior first-five-nests burst or 2/45s bursts. Still max **2 owned supports per trainer** and no unsafe forced on-camera placement. Co-op support-spawn cadence and the competitive Egg Rush scoring cadence (60s) are unchanged.
4. **Orb:** normal-enemy drop probability **3% → 6%**; retain existing Orb eligibility (learned-move progress), FIFO handling, run-only maximum 10, and distinct boss special roll. Do not buff personal ticket/Shiny rates.
5. **Support HP in Sống Còn and Sống Sót Co-op:** Each hatched support has own max HP **50–80% of owner's max HP**, not a cosmetic fake. Show always-visible **28×3 px** mini bar at sprite head, full or damaged, both modes. Host applies hostile enemy-contact damage (1.25s cooldown, capped to 20% max HP per hit), shield blocking, support faints/remove from owned capacity and sends `er:hp` with monotonic per-pet sequence plus `er:down`. Host also sends healed support HP; guests render Host state. Competitive Egg Rush pets keep original non-PvP rules.
6. **Deaths inside Rift:** Upstream Rift exit used `if (pl.dead) pl.revive(0.3)`. This now applies **only to solo runs**; in Royale, zero HP means elimination even inside the Rift and never resets to 30% HP on return. Co-op fallen players remain down so teammates can rescue them under Co-op rules, rather than receiving a free Rift-exit revive.
7. Retain all previous v1.5.31 gameplay: Egg Rush 6 personal / 10 shared eggs finish, immediate last-survivor Royale result, full ending rankings with exact Pokémon sprite/Shiny, host-owned competitive bot stat balance, Web AI hotfix against 5s reload, independently scrolling home menu.

## Follow-up: real companion movement in all three multiplayer modes

- **Sống Sót Co-op, Sống Còn and Tranh Trứng** share `src/web/pkmsvv-egg-rush.js`; all hatched companions now follow their **own trainer's distance-sampled trail** (waypoints every >=8 world pixels), as Solo's `src/v15/eggs.js` does.
- Each companion moves at a world-space speed capped to **85% of its owner's move speed**, with terrain collision, 5-pixel rest deadzone and return to `Idle` when blocked or stopped. Real `G.Sprites.Animator` plays **`Walk`** using actual measured velocity, orientation with `G.Sprites.dirFromAngle`, speed-linked frame playback and **`Idle`** when stationary. No per-frame position lerp that makes the sprites slide.
- Companion sprite scale stays **60% of normal Pokémon scale**, including the normal big-sprite adjustment. Applies to all modes, not just Solo.
- When entering/exiting Rift or a large world teleport, the follower trail and companions re-anchor near their owner; teleport does not generate a walking animation/sprint.
- New `tests/lan-support-animation.test.cjs` exercises walking animation, real movement speed cap, idle after stopping, collision, own-trainer pairing and warp recovery in **each of the three modes**. Included in PC, Web and Android CI scripts, not just manual QA.
