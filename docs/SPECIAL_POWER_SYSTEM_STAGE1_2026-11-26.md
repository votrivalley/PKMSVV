# Special Power System — approved design, Stage 1

Approved: one pre-run power per Pokémon, existing Mega Stone ownership and Arena Points preserved; eventually shared Power Gacha, shared research upgrades, one activation input, and minute-20 Eternamax awakening choices. Pippi and Fusion independent. Mega currently 30 seconds / 200 cooldown; proposed 40s is deferred until common controller validation. Dynamax 12/80, Z-Power 15/60, Terastal 30/75. This QA build implements an actual power selection screen replacing only the Research Lab Mega entry; old Mega catalog accessible. Per-species choice persists in pkmsvvPowerLoadout using existing commit; absent or invalid choice defaults Mega. Dynamax trial requires Chapter 7, solo Explore and shared 100 charge; no grant of permanent equipment, Arena spend, G-Max art, enlarged collider, prop breaking, AoE, Z-Move or Tera effects yet. Real HP x2 and attack x1.4, proportional HP changes, cooldown after stop, no stacking with legacy Mega; pause timer during choices, cleanup on evolution/death/exit. Co-op/Arena remain legacy Mega until replication and balance tested. Existing room-flash QA failed due route unregistration race, corrected by waiting for route handlers. Unit and integrated browser QA pending; no public or Beta deployment.
