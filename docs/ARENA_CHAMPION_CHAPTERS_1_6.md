# Arena / Champion v1.5.44 — 2026-10-29

Chapter numbering in user-facing UI is now **1 Awakening, 2 Memory Fragments, 3 Eighth Echo, 4 Forgotten Life, 5 Companions, 6 Crown Arena**. The old internal keys (prologue, chapter1, chapter2, chapter3) are kept to preserve all existing saves and previous reward ledger entries 0–3. Chapters 5 and 6 are bilingual cutscenes triggered by opening Multiplayer for the first time after Chapter 4 and Arena for the first time after Chapter 5. They can be replayed in Memories and each pays exactly one additional purple x10 ticket (ledger 4/5); no duplicate old payouts.

Arena entry is in the same main menu, 10 selectable regions, every tournament starts with 16 bracket entries (player+15 AI). At least two AIs are elite; the remaining bots vary. Other AI-vs-AI bracket matches are simulated; there are **four real player boss duels**, which each require killing an actual G.Enemy actor with combat damage in the live game. Between them, a 180-second preparation countdown offers 'Ready now'. Duels time out after 150 seconds. A fifth **real reigning Champion boss duel** follows the final. The tournament cup remains earned even on Champion loss; its regional Crown Seal only follows a real Champion kill. Ten seals are tracked for future Chapter 10 (not claimed or enabled in this build).

Tournament first-place gets 10 yellow single Gacha tickets, second 6, third 3; entrants receive PokéCoins. The best outcome per region is recorded in save.arenas.pkmChampion, with upgrades only; refresh/replay doesn't farm another same-rank payout. Uses the original Enemy AI and normal species sprites; eight unready Mega sprite forms are NOT misrepresented as playable Mega Champions. Mega battle and multiplayer LAN capacity are untouched. No network server for tournament. Do not claim multiplayer 16-player fights; 16 is the single-player bracket, real fights are 1v1 and other matches simulated.
